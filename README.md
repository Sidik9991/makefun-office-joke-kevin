# The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head

Entry for the make.fun bounty [The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c): one line, at most 200 characters — the funniest joke about office life, judged head to head.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) scores 11 fresh candidate lines against the judge's five criteria (one-line office joke with a turn, universal office truth, surprise, punchline last, fresh), picks the winner, writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and validates the 1–200 character one-line gate.

**The line (73 chars):**

> My calendar is so full I booked a meeting with myself, and I declined it.

Why this one: one concrete office scene (the overfull calendar), a flat insider setup, and the punch lands on the last clause — declining your own meeting. No staged theatre, no narrating the joke's own effect, and the shortest of the three lines tied at the top score, which the bounty tie-break itself favours.
